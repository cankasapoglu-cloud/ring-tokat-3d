How do I optimize my thumbnails and titles for more views?
Create thumbnails that speak to your target audience—familiar visuals for subscribers and clear actions or emotions for new viewers. Keep designs simple, readable, and eye-catching across devices, and stay up to date with what resonates in your community. Don’t be afraid to refresh older thumbnails to attract new viewers.
Make sure titles accurately reflect your video and get to the point quickly. Use important keywords early, avoid overusing caps or emojis, and choose between searchable titles or curiosity-driven ones based on how viewers might discover your content.
Use YouTube Analytics to see what your audience is searching for, which videos they watch, and how your thumbnails and titles perform. Track click-through rate (CTR) to understand what’s working for subscribers and new viewers, and adjust over time.

How do I get my videos to appear in YouTube Search?
On YouTube, viewers are matched to the videos they are most likely to watch and enjoy. YouTube’s search and discovery system caters to each viewer and their varying interests. The goal is to connect each viewer with videos they want to watch so that they come back to YouTube regularly.
To get your videos seen, we recommend focusing on what your audience likes. If you do that and people watch, the recommendations will follow. Ask yourself, which videos does your audience enjoy most? How often do they like to watch my channel? To help you with these questions, be sure to get to know your audience .
